Who we are

For 35 years, the people behind PFG have worked at the centre of tech and innovation ecosystems around the world.

We lean into complexity and cross borders to unlock the capital our partners need to grow. Founded in 2004, PFG has become a specialist growth lender with a team spread across six offices.

What we do

Flexible growth debt and asset-backed finance, built around each company's next stage.

We work closely with founders and finance teams to shape structures that speed up the pivotal moments: term loans, lines of credit, royalty or revenue-based finance, and warehouse facilities for lenders.

Loans of $1 to 50MM for companies with $5 to 150MM in revenue, or asset portfolios of $5MM and up.

How growth debt works
Strategic partnerships

Long partnerships that let us lend further and deeper.

30+ year partner

SVB

An official strategic partnership since the late 1980s. PFG and SVB have lent side by side across the US and Canada, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and Latin America, and co-manage a Latin America Growth Lending Fund.

Co-investment programme

IFC

A strategic co-investment program with IFC, part of the World Bank Group, widens PFG's sourcing, adds diligence depth and extends its reach into fintech and tech-enabled businesses in growth markets.

10+ year partner

IDB Invest

PFG leads a joint venture with IDB Invest, delivering growth-stage credit to Latin American tech companies through the Latin America Growth Lending Fund, picking up where early venture funding leaves off.

Ecosystem

DIFC

An affiliation with the Dubai International Financial Centre, the hub linking the Middle East, Africa and South Asia, where founders and investors build and scale.
