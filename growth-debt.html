What we do

Tailored debt for the turning points in a company's growth.

We do growth debt

Call it venture debt if you like. We think the point is growth.

“Venture debt” is the usual shorthand for lending to private tech companies. PFG's view is broader: financing exists to help a business grow, so that is how we design every facility.

Like venture debt, growth debt is cheaper than equity and limits dilution. It can fund product, sales and marketing, new markets, or a buffer for the unexpected. The differences are in when it is offered, how it is assessed and the shape it takes.
