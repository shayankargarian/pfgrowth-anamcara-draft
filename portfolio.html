Featured stories

Six financings, in brief.

Middle East & North Africa · Fintech

Tabby

A fast-growing marketplace and BNPL app with an established receivables book needed a facility to grow lending, which freed capital for merchants, product and customers.

Latin America · Software & services

SUMA

A finance and compliance platform for accountants and small businesses used growth debt to expand in Mexico and pursue acquisitions across the region.

Australia & New Zealand · Consumer & e-commerce

Koala

A bootstrapped Australian e-commerce leader with a strong record wanted to move into new products and markets before raising institutional equity.

Asia Pacific · Fintech

Kredivo

A consumer digital credit card business needed a bespoke offshore warehouse facility to fund a fast-growing loan book.

North America · Healthcare

divvyDOSE

A founder-backed specialty pharmacy improving patient outcomes sought an alternative to venture capital to keep growing quickly.

Australia & New Zealand · Software & services

HotDoc

A Melbourne healthcare SaaS company with strong top-line growth, still investing ahead of cash flow, used a recurring revenue facility to scale subscriptions.
